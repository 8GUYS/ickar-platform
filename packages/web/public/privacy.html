ICKAR Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use ICKAR, we collect:

  • Account information: Your name and email address when you register.
  • Payment information: Payments are processed by Paystack. We store only your subscription status — never your card details.
  • Usage data: Which titles you access, to manage your library and entitlements.
  • Device data: Device type and OS version for crash reporting and compatibility.
  • Push notification token: To send you new title alerts (you can opt out anytime).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide access to your subscribed content
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To send notifications about new content (with your permission)
  • To improve the platform via anonymised analytics
  • To detect and prevent fraud

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Paystack — payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Google Firebase — crash reporting (anonymised)
  • Supabase — secure database hosting (EU/US data centres)
  • Cloudflare — content delivery

4. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by emailing support@ickar.ng. We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

ICKAR is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Audio streams use signed, time-limited URLs (15-minute expiry) to prevent unauthorised access. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@ickar.ng.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We'll notify you via the app or email for significant changes.
